# MBS disclosure analytics rules

- Preserve native source grain and as-of timing; issuance, security-period, loan-period, supplemental, and daily reports are not interchangeable.
- Treat provider schema/layout changes, consolidations, corrections, and retirements as lineage events, not economic events.
- Keep original-publication and latest-known views; never overwrite restatement evidence.
- Reconcile beginning balance, issuance/adjustments, principal reductions, removals, and ending balance before publishing runoff claims.
- Do not label total balance decline as voluntary prepayment. Separate scheduled principal, curtailments, voluntary payoff, involuntary removal, correction, and termination when supported.
- Publish SMM, CPR, PSA, roll, cure, modification, delinquency, and concentration rates only with approved eligible populations, timing, denominators, exclusions, and formula versions.
- Keep Classic FICO and VS4 distinct. State whether credit, LTV, CLTV, ELTV, rate, age, and maturity attributes are original, issuance, updated, or current.
- Do not infer valuation, price, yield, spread, duration, convexity, return, refinance incentive, loss severity, or causal behavior from disclosure files alone.
- Treat seller, servicer, geography, borrower, property, mission, green, and social attributes as descriptive portfolio characteristics, not borrower decisions or impact claims.
- Restricted security/loan identifiers and row-level fields stay inside the authorized boundary and never enter public artifacts, screenshots, logs, or AI context.
